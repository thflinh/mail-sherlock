How it works

Three steps to find any email

No setup beyond connecting your accounts. No keywords, no folders, no filters.

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in with Google once per account. Smart Inbox reads your emails securely via the Gmail API.

2

We index your inbox

Your emails are converted to AI embeddings locally on your machine. Nothing is sent to a server.

3

Just describe it

Type what you remember in plain English. The model finds the closest matches by meaning, not words.

Features

Everything your inbox was missing

Built for people with busy, multi-account inboxes who need results fast.

Multi-account search

Search Gmail, personal, and school accounts simultaneously with one query.

Semantic search

Finds emails by meaning, not exact words. Works even when you misremember the phrasing.

100% private

Embeddings are built and stored locally. Your emails never leave your device.

Smart auto-tags

Emails are automatically labeled — finance, work, housing, travel — for faster filtering.

Chrome extension

Works directly in your browser as a popup or sidebar. No separate app needed.

Relevance scoring

Every result shows a match percentage so you always know why it was surfaced.
